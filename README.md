# Spider Peers — get paid for your spare bandwidth

Run one lightweight agent and turn the internet connection you already pay for
into monthly income. Install it, set a cap, and it quietly shares spare
bandwidth in the background — you get paid for every GB you serve.

### → Start earning at **[peers.spider.cloud](https://peers.spider.cloud)**

## How it works

1. **Install the agent** — macOS, Android, or an Android TV / Fire TV. One app,
   runs quietly in the background.
2. **Share spare bandwidth** — set a cap so it never saturates your line, and
   block any sites you don't want in your pool. You stay in control.
3. **Get paid monthly** — bandwidth is metered server-side and paid out in cash.
   Your rate is agreed per peer based on connection quality and volume.

## Easy setup on a TV

No keyboard needed: the app shows a short code and a QR. Scan it (or open
**[peers.spider.cloud/link](https://peers.spider.cloud/link)**) and approve from
your phone — that's it. See **[peers.spider.cloud/tv](https://peers.spider.cloud/tv)**.

## You stay in control

- **Bandwidth cap** you set — never saturates your connection.
- **Block-list firewall** — refuse to proxy any domain you choose.
- **Private by design** — your browser history is read locally only, to protect
  your own sites; it never leaves your machine.
